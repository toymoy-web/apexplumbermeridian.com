If you smell gas

Natural gas has an added odor often compared to rotten eggs, so leaks can be noticed. Treat any strong smell as an emergency.

  • Leave the house and take everyone with you.
  • Do not turn lights or appliances on or off, and do not use a phone indoors.
  • Do not try to find the leak yourself.
  • From a safe distance, call Intermountain Gas, the utility serving Meridian.
  • Once the utility has made the area safe, call us to repair piping on your side of the meter.

The utility handles the meter and the line up to it. Piping from the meter into and through your home is the owner's responsibility.

Who is allowed to work on gas lines in Idaho

Idaho licenses fuel gas piping separately from plumbing. Gas piping falls under the state's HVAC licensing, which includes a specialty license specifically for fuel gas piping, and, according to the board's rules, gas work follows the International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC) as adopted in Idaho.

That means gas line jobs booked through our line are handled by technicians licensed for fuel gas work, with the appropriate permit and inspection. We say this plainly because a general plumbing license alone does not cover gas piping in this state.

Why every gas job ends with a pressure test

Before a new or repaired gas line is put into service, it is pressure tested to prove it holds without leaking. The test uses air or inert gas at a set pressure held for a set time, and the inspector confirms it.

Soap solution on fittings finds small leaks the test reveals. Only after the line passes are appliances connected and lit. This step is not optional, and it is the main reason gas work should never be improvised.

What affects the cost of gas line work

Gas work is priced by the length of pipe, the access, and the permit.

  • Distance from the meter or nearest branch to the appliance.
  • Pipe size needed for the appliance's gas demand.
  • Access through the crawl space, attic, or finished walls.
  • Exterior or buried runs for outdoor appliances.
  • Permit, pressure test, and inspection.

Questions before you call

Gas line questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave immediately, avoid switches and phones inside, and call Intermountain Gas from outside. Call us for repairs once it is safe.

Can a plumber work on gas lines in Idaho?

Idaho licenses fuel gas piping under its HVAC licensing, including a fuel gas piping specialty. Our gas jobs go to technicians licensed for that work.

Do I need a permit to add a gas line?

Yes. New gas piping is permitted and inspected, and it must pass a pressure test before use.

Why does a tankless heater need a bigger gas line?

Tankless units burn much more gas at full output than a tank heater, so the existing pipe may be too small.

Who is responsible for the gas meter?

Intermountain Gas handles the meter and its supply line. Piping on your side of the meter is the owner's responsibility.

Can you run gas to an outdoor grill?

Yes. Outdoor lines need proper burial or protection, a shutoff, and a pressure test.

Can you hook up a gas fireplace or convert an appliance to propane?

We run and test the gas line to a fireplace insert, range, or grill. Converting an appliance between natural gas and propane needs the manufacturer's conversion kit and is done to the appliance's instructions.

Can you restore gas service after Intermountain Gas shuts off the meter?

We repair the leak in your piping and pressure test it, available 24/7. Once it passes, the utility is called back to restore service at the meter.
