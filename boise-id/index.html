Boise neighborhoods we reach first, corridor by corridor

Boise is Idaho's capital and largest city, with 235,684 residents at the 2020 census. We focus on the part of it closest to Meridian: the west side along Chinden, Ustick, Fairview, Franklin, and Overland, the southwest side out toward the airport, and the Bench.

These areas grew outward from the city in waves. Much of west Boise filled in from the 1970s through the 2000s, while the Boise Bench closer to downtown holds older homes from the postwar decades. That mix decides what usually fails.

Boise is the seat of Ada County, the same county as Meridian, so the codes, inspectors, and winter conditions will feel familiar on both sides of the city line.

Emergency dispatch for Boise runs around the clock, and the drive from Meridian to the Boise Towne Square area on Milwaukee Street is a short hop east, even late at night.

Burst pipe or active leak in Boise? Do this first

Boise homes range from slab ranches on the Bench to two-story houses over crawl spaces in the west, so the shutoff is not always where you expect.

Find the main shutoff

Check the garage wall where the water line enters, a utility closet, or the crawl space. If it will not budge, the curb stop in the meter box near the street shuts off the lot.

Cut power to wet areas

Turn off breakers for rooms where water reaches outlets or fixtures, and switch a gas water heater to pilot if the main will be off for a while.

Protect floors and belongings

Lift rugs, electronics, and boxes out of the water path and take photos before cleanup for your insurer.

Call us, then your water provider if needed

Reach our 24/7 line at (208) 679-8357. For a break at the meter or in the street, call the water provider named on your bill.

Boise has its own water provider and permit office

Most Boise addresses get water from Veolia, the private utility that serves much of the city, rather than from a city-run system like Meridian's. The provider's annual water quality report is the one that applies to your tap, so check the name on your bill before choosing a softener or filter.

Plumbing permits inside Boise city limits come from the City of Boise rather than Meridian Building Services. Water heater replacements, sewer repairs, and repipes are permitted work either way, and the plumber pulls the right permit for your address.

When a Boise call is an emergency, and when it can wait

Sewage coming up through a tub or floor drain, water spraying from a pipe, and a water heater leaking from the tank are emergencies at any hour. Close the main or stop using water and call.

A slow sink, a running toilet, or a water heater that recovers slowly can usually wait for a daytime visit, which costs less. If you are not sure, call and describe it, and we will tell you honestly which it is. For a heater that quit outright, our water heater repair page covers the common causes.
