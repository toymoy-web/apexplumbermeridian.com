Eagle neighborhoods and their plumbing

Eagle was a small river town of about 3,300 people as late as 1990. It incorporated in 1971, and its first wave of subdivisions grew up around Eagle Hills Golf Course and Banbury in the late 1970s and 1980s. Those homes are now 40 or more years old, and their original copper lines, water heaters, and fixtures are the source of many of our Eagle calls.

Growth took off after 1990. Eagle reached 11,085 residents in 2000 and 30,346 by 2020, with large custom and luxury subdivisions such as Spurwing and Two Rivers. Bigger homes bring bigger plumbing: multiple water heaters, recirculation pumps, long runs to far bathrooms, and whole-house water treatment.

Downtown Eagle along State Street mixes older commercial buildings with new mixed-use construction, so commercial and restaurant plumbing matters there as much as residential.

Emergency dispatch runs around the clock for Eagle homes and businesses, reached from Meridian by Eagle Road and Linder Road.

Eagle sits in Ada County, with parts of the city stretching into Boise and Gem counties, so the permit office and utility depend on exactly where your lot falls. A recirculation pump, a second water heater, or a softener on the main line are the pieces most worth checking in larger Eagle homes.

Before We Arrive: If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Eagle

Larger Eagle homes often have more than one water heater and longer runs of pipe, which means more places to check and more water that can escape. Start with these steps.

Shut off the main valve

Find the main in the crawl space, mechanical room, or garage. In homes near the river, check the crawl space for standing water before going in.

Kill power to wet areas

Turn off breakers for flooded rooms and for any electric water heater or recirculation pump near the leak.

Clear the immediate area

Lift rugs, electronics, and furniture off wet floors. Finished basements and bonus rooms over garages need attention first.

Eagle has more than one water provider

Unlike Meridian, Eagle does not have a single water utility. Most homes were long served by the private Eagle Water Company, which was sold to SUEZ after a 2021 settlement with the city and is now part of Veolia. The City of Eagle runs its own municipal system for a smaller group of customers, and some rural lots rely on private wells.

That matters when something leaks. Who owns the meter, whose water quality report applies, and who to call for a problem at the street all depend on the name on your bill. Check it before you call so we can point you to the right place for anything on the utility side.

Living near the river changes the crawl space

Eagle grew up on Eagle Island and the land between the Boise River's north and south channels. The ground there is fertile because it has always been close to the water, and the water table near the river runs high.

For a home over a crawl space, that can mean damp soil and higher humidity underneath the house. A small plumbing leak adds to the moisture and can go unnoticed longer. If you smell mustiness or see condensation on crawl space pipes, have the lines checked. Our leak detection service can confirm whether a pipe is part of the problem.

Eagle Island State Park, 545 acres between the channels and once a state prison honor farm, is a reminder of how much of this land was farmed, irrigated, and wet long before the subdivisions came.

Where groundwater does reach the crawl space, a properly sized pit and pump is the usual fix, and our sump pump repair page covers how those systems are tested before irrigation season.

Eagle plumbing questions

Is emergency service available in Eagle?

Yes. Our line is answered around the clock for Eagle, and after-hours calls for leaks, sewer backups, and heaters that quit are dispatched the same night when needed.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Eagle?

Eagle has one ZIP code, 83616, and we cover all of it. That takes in downtown along State Street, the Eagle Hills and Banbury neighborhoods, the river subdivisions around Eagle Island State Park, and the foothill lots that cross into Gem County.

Who is my water provider in Eagle?

My hot water takes forever to reach the far bathroom. Can you fix that?

Do homes near the Boise River have special plumbing issues?

Can you service a home with two water heaters?

Do you work on Eagle homes with private wells?
